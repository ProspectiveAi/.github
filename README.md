# Prospective

**Advancing frontier intelligence and privacy-preserving AI.**

Prospective is an independent research and engineering laboratory building next-generation artificial intelligence models. Our mission is to ensure that advanced AI capabilities remain fundamentally private, verifiable, and user-controlled.

---

## Core Models

### 🧠 SCAI — Cognitive Intelligence
Our proprietary family of language and multimodal models engineered for deep reasoning, natural dialogue, and secure context processing without compromising user privacy.
* **`SCAI 1.0 Inception`** *(Flagship Baseline)* — The foundational generation of our neural architecture, deployed across secure environments including partner platforms like SChat.

---

## Principles

1. **Privacy by Architecture:** Confidentiality is enforced at the computational and protocol layers, not added as an afterthought.
2. **Perspective & Foresight:** We design resilient AI systems built for long-term reliability and digital sovereignty.
3. **Intelligent Utility:** AI should amplify human thinking while respecting strict boundaries of data ownership.
