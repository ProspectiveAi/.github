# Prospective

**Advancing secure communications and frontier intelligence.**

Prospective is an independent research and engineering laboratory building privacy-first communication infrastructure and next-generation artificial intelligence models. Our mission is to ensure that advanced AI capabilities and personal digital correspondence remain fundamentally private, verifiable, and user-controlled.

---

## Core Ecosystem

### 🛡️ SChat — Safety Chat
A decentralized, censorship-resistant messenger engineered for uncompromising privacy and anonymity.
* **Tor-Routed Architecture:** All network traffic is routed natively through the Tor network, obscuring IP addresses, metadata, and physical locations by design.
* **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE):** Cryptographic protection ensures that messages, media, and calls can only be decrypted by the intended participants—never by servers or third parties.
* **Zero-Knowledge Principles:** Built with minimal metadata retention and local-first security controls.

### 🧠 SCAI — Cognitive Intelligence
Our proprietary family of language and multimodal models integrated directly into the SChat ecosystem to provide intelligent assistance without compromising user privacy.
* **`SCAI 1.0 Inception`** *(Flagship Baseline)* — The foundational generation of our neural architecture, optimized for reasoning, natural dialogue, and secure context processing.

---

## Principles

1. **Privacy by Architecture:** Security is not an optional feature; it is enforced at the protocol and cryptographic layers.
2. **Perspective & Foresight:** We design resilient systems built for long-term digital sovereignty.
3. **Intelligent Utility:** AI should amplify human communication while respecting strict boundaries of confidentiality.
